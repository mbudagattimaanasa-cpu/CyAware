Responsible AI Commitment

How CyAware ensures fair, transparent, and non-punitive digital moderation.

Context Matters: Words Require Situational Review

Language is inherently nuanced. A single detected message does not constitute definitive proof of cyberbullying without contextual evaluation. CyAware operates strictly as an assistive intelligence layer. The platform does not enforce automated punishments, account suspensions, or automated bans. Final determinations always remain with human moderators.

Contextual Nuance

Evaluating conversational patterns and dialogue relationships rather than relying on blunt keyword blacklists.

Standardized Taxonomy

Agreed Classification Categories

The CyAware classification model is aligned to five standardized categories to ensure consistent evaluation.

Normal

Normal

Constructive communication, benign banter, and everyday conversational exchanges without abusive intent.

Insult

Insult

Disparaging, demeaning, or personally offensive remarks intended to belittle an individual's character or appearance.

Threat

Threat

Declarations of intent to inflict physical, emotional, or psychological harm, coercion, or violent intimidation.

Hate Speech

Hate Speech

Attacks, vilification, or dehumanizing language directed against protected identities, religion, race, gender, or disability.

Toxic / Abusive

Toxic Language

Excessively hostile, vulgar, profane, or aggressively antagonistic discourse that degrades communicative safety.

* Note: These definitions represent the model's standardized classification framework. No synthetic or unverified predictions are shown.
