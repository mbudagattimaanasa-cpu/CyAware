Responsible AI & Assistive Governance

CyAware enforces a Context-First Assistive Model. Detection algorithms classify linguistic features into five defined categories (Normal, Insult, Threat, Hate Speech, Toxic/Abusive Language), but automated findings do not constitute final proof of cyberbullying.

All outputs are designed strictly for assistive decision support by human moderators, educators, and guardians. CyAware prohibits automated punitive actions without contextual human review.
