Responsible AI & Ethical Governance Charter

CyAware operates as an assistive intelligence layer. Language requires context: a single detected phrase does not constitute definitive proof of cyberbullying without comprehensive human review.

Automated punitive or blocking actions are strictly avoided. All classification outputs are advisory signals intended for educators, moderators, and human investigators.
