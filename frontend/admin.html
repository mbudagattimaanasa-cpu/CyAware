Responsible AI & Ethical Governance Policy

Administrative decisions must not rely exclusively on automated classifiers. Detection models classify text into five linguistic categories (Normal, Insult, Threat, Hate Speech, Toxic/Abusive Language), but automated findings represent assistive signals for human review.

CyAware strictly prohibits automated disciplinary, punitive, or blocking actions without comprehensive situational review by human administrators, educators, and moderators.
